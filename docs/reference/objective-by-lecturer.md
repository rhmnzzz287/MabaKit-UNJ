BUILD A DIGITAL BUSINESS WEBSITE
Objective

The objective of this project is to apply the concepts learned in Digital Entrepreneurship by developing a website for a digital business idea. Students are expected to identify a customer problem, develop a value proposition, and translate the business idea into a website that demonstrates how customers interact with the business.



Instructions

Choose any product, service, or digital business idea. There are no restrictions on the business category.
Define a clear target customer and customer problem before developing the website.
Build the website using Lovable AI, another AI website builder, or your own code using React, Next.js, Python, or other suitable technologies.
The website must be publicly accessible through a URL.
The website should demonstrate the main customer journey, from discovering the business to taking the desired action.
Record a Customer Journey Video explaining how a customer interacts with your website.
Submit all required outputs before the deadline.
Business Concept

Your website should be built around a clear business idea.

Define:

Customer Problem
What specific problem or need does your business address?

Target Customer
Who is the website designed for?

Business Idea
What product, service, or solution are you offering?

Value Proposition
Why should customers choose your business?

Revenue Model
How does the business make money?

Minimum Website Requirements

The website should include:

Homepage
Product or Service Catalog
Product or Service Detail Page
Main Transaction or Conversion Page
About Us Page
The exact pages may be adjusted depending on the type of business. For example, a booking business may use a Booking Page instead of a Shopping Cart. A digital service may use a Service Selection or Registration Page.



Additional Requirements

Minimum of 5 products, services, or offerings.
All navigation, links, and main buttons should function properly.
Product or service information should include relevant images, descriptions, and prices or other applicable information.
Include clear CTA buttons such as Shop Now, Book Now, Sign Up, Get Started, or other relevant actions.
The website should have consistent branding, visuals, and business information.
The website must be accessible through a public URL.
Customer Journey Video

Maximum 7 minutes

The video should demonstrate one complete customer journey through your website.

The video should include:

Entering the homepage.
Exploring the product or service offering.
Selecting one product or service.
Viewing the relevant detail page.
Taking the main action, such as purchasing, booking, registering, or submitting an inquiry.
Explaining how the website supports the customer's journey.
Showing the final outcome of the customer journey.
Video Format

Students do not need to appear on camera.
Not all group members need to appear in the video.
The video can use screen recording with narration or voice-over.
One or more group members may appear on camera if preferred.
The focus should be on demonstrating the website and explaining the customer journey.
Outputs

Public Website URL
A functional website that represents your digital business idea.

Customer Journey Video
Maximum 7 minutes. The video should demonstrate one complete customer journey and explain how the website supports the customer's needs.
Assessment Criteria

Website Completeness	30%
Business Concept	40%
Customer Journey Video	30%


Assessment Indicators

Website Completeness, 30%

The website has all the required pages and features.
All menus, links, and main buttons work properly.
The website has at least five products, services, or offerings.
The website can be accessed through a public URL.
Users can complete the main action on the website, such as buying, booking, registering, or submitting an inquiry.
Business Concept, 40%

The website clearly explains what the business is about.
It is clear who the target customers are.
The problem and solution are easy to understand.
The products or services match the business idea.
The pricing, branding, images, and information are consistent.
Customer Journey Video, 30%

The video shows a complete customer journey from entering the website to completing the main action.
The video clearly explains what the customer is doing at each step.
The video shows how the website helps the customer.
The video is clear, organized, and easy to follow
